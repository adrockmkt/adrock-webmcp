# v0.6.8 Grounding Benchmark Protocol

Status: protocolo congelado antes da execução.

Date: 2026-09-25.

## Objective

Measure whether v0.6 Content Retrieval closes the grounding limitation observed in v0.5, especially the C-Q2 case where discovery found the correct article but the final answer attributed details that were not present in the metadata returned by `search_blog`.

This benchmark evaluates grounding after retrieval. It does not re-evaluate search-engine ranking and does not claim general superiority of WebMCP.

## Hypothesis

> When an agent uses `search_blog` followed by `get_blog_article`, substantive answers about Ad Rock content remain supported by the retrieved public article body and avoid material extrapolation beyond that content.

## Comparison

Use two WebMCP conditions.

### D. Discovery only

Expose the validated `search_blog` behavior but do not use article-body retrieval when answering.

Purpose: preserve a metadata-only baseline corresponding to the limitation identified in v0.5.

### E. Discovery + retrieval

Expose the production WebMCP tools and allow the agent to choose them automatically.

Expected observable flow for substantive questions:

`search_blog(query) -> get_blog_article(canonical slug) -> final answer`

Do not manually select a tool or manually rewrite the tool parameters.

## Frozen questions

Use substantive questions where the answer requires more than identifying that an article exists.

1. What does Ad Rock recommend for GA4 auditing?
2. According to Ad Rock, how should companies prepare GA4 measurement for MCP and AI?
3. What does Ad Rock say changes for technical SEO with AI Search?
4. According to Ad Rock, how should Google Ads conversion tracking be configured with GA4?

The wording must remain unchanged between D and E.

## Execution controls

For every question and condition:

- start a fresh agent session;
- use the same agent/model where operationally possible;
- record model and execution date;
- do not provide the expected URL to the agent;
- do not manually invoke WebMCP tools;
- do not correct search parameters;
- do not reuse prior answers as context;
- preserve infrastructure/model failures as failures or explicit retries;
- record only observable actions, not hidden reasoning.

Initial benchmark size:

- 4 questions;
- 2 conditions;
- 8 primary executions.

A retry is permitted only for a documented transient infrastructure/model failure and must not replace the original record.

## Required evidence per execution

Record:

- condition;
- question;
- final answer;
- URLs/sources used;
- `search_blog` calls and parameters;
- `get_blog_article` calls and slugs;
- article(s) retrieved;
- observable tool-call order;
- errors/retries;
- screenshot or trace when available.

Do not preserve private model chain-of-thought or opaque thought signatures.

## Metrics

### correct_article_found

Binary.

- 1: the primary reference or another materially appropriate article is discovered.
- 0: no materially appropriate article is discovered.

### primary_article_found

Binary.

- 1: the frozen primary reference is discovered.
- 0: it is not discovered.

### article_body_retrieved

Binary.

- 1: the normalized body of a materially appropriate article is retrieved with `get_blog_article`.
- 0: no appropriate article body is retrieved.

For condition D this should normally be 0 by design.

### retrieval_contract

Scale 0 to 2.

- 2: retrieval uses a canonical slug returned by discovery and retrieves the corresponding approved artifact.
- 1: retrieval succeeds but requires an additional discovery/retry step.
- 0: invalid/unknown slug, wrong artifact, arbitrary URL behavior or no successful retrieval when retrieval is applicable.
- N/A: retrieval is not available in the condition.

### grounding_support

Scale 0 to 2. This is the primary outcome metric.

- 2: all material claims attributed to Ad Rock are supported by the retrieved article content.
- 1: the answer is mostly supported but contains a minor unsupported inference or overstatement.
- 0: at least one material attributed claim is absent from or contradicted by the retrieved content.

For condition D, evaluate support against the actual metadata/context returned to the agent, not against article content the agent did not receive.

### unsupported_attribution

Binary.

- 0: no material unsupported attribution to Ad Rock.
- 1: at least one material unsupported attribution is present.

This explicitly tracks the v0.5 C-Q2 failure mode.

### source_accuracy

Scale 0 to 2.

- 2: cited/used source exists and materially supports the answer.
- 1: source exists but support is partial.
- 0: invented, nonexistent or materially unrelated source.

### answer_completeness

Scale 0 to 2.

- 2: directly and substantively answers the question.
- 1: useful but materially incomplete.
- 0: does not materially answer the question.

### tool_calls

Count observable WebMCP calls.

Also record the sequence, for example:

`search_blog -> get_blog_article`

Do not create a composite score from these metrics.

## Evaluation rule

The evaluator must judge attributed claims against only the information actually made available to that execution.

For condition E, use the exact normalized artifact returned by `get_blog_article` as the grounding reference.

A statement may be a reasonable general-domain inference and still count as unsupported if the answer presents it specifically as something Ad Rock says or recommends and the retrieved article does not support it.

Paraphrase is allowed. Verbatim overlap is not required.

## Gate criteria

v0.6 retrieval is supported for progression to 0.6.9 when the observed E executions satisfy all of the following:

1. appropriate article body retrieved in 4/4 questions;
2. retrieval contract score 2 in 4/4 questions;
3. grounding support score 2 in at least 3/4 questions;
4. no more than 1/4 E answers contains an unsupported attribution;
5. no security/regression failure is observed during the benchmark.

These thresholds are frozen before execution and must not be relaxed after seeing results.

A failure to meet them is a valid result and must be documented rather than repaired by changing the benchmark after execution.

## Interpretation constraints

The benchmark can support claims about this POC, these questions, this corpus and the tested agent/model.

It does not establish:

- universal behavior across agents;
- improvement to Google/Bing ranking;
- statistical significance;
- that retrieval eliminates every hallucination;
- that WebMCP replaces general web search or RAG;
- that metadata-only discovery is always inferior for simple existence questions.
